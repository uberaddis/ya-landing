## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect information you provide directly, information generated automatically during your use of the Services, and information from third-party sources.

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Registration Data:** When you sign up as a Rider or Driver, we collect your name, phone number, email address, password, profile photo, and language preferences.
- **Payment Details:** For in-app wallet funding and card payments, we collect payment card info, digital wallet numbers, or transaction receipts.
- **Driver Compliance & Verification Data:** We collect documents required to verify Drivers, including national ID card or passport, driver’s license, vehicle registration papers (blue card), vehicle insurance documents, and background check reports.
- **User Communications:** We collect information when you contact our customer support team or send messages to other users (Rider-to-Driver) via the in-app chat function.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Real-Time Location Data:**
  - **Riders:** We collect precise or approximate location data from your mobile device when the Rider App is running in the foreground. If you permit background location access, we may also collect location data when the app is closed or not in use.
  - **Drivers:** We collect precise location data from your mobile device when the Driver App is open and running in the foreground or background (including when your status is set to "online" or "active"). This is required to match you with riders and calculate trip distances.
- **Transaction Information:** We collect details of trips and deliveries, including pick-up and drop-off addresses, dates, times, distance traveled, fares charged, and payment methods.
- **Device & Usage Information:** We collect information about the hardware and software you use, including device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, mobile network operator, and app crash logs.
- **Cookies & Tracking Technologies:** We use cookies and similar technologies on our website and in-app web views to store user preferences and track analytics.

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your personal information to deliver a safe, reliable, and efficient service. Specifically, we use your data to:

- **Provide and Maintain Services:** Create and manage your account, match Riders with Drivers, map routes, calculate fares, and process payments.
- **Ensure Safety and Security:** Verify identities and eligibility, monitor trips in real-time, detect fraud or suspicious behavior, and enforce our Terms of Service.
- **Customer Support:** Investigate, troubleshoot, and resolve dispute cases or technical issues.
- **Communications:** Send transaction messages (receipts, booking confirmations), push notifications (trip updates), and operational alerts (verification updates).
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Comply with local transport directives issued by the **Addis Ababa Transport Bureau (AATB)** and cybersecurity requirements mandated by the **Information Network Security Administration (INSA)** of Ethiopia.

## 3. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only under the following circumstances:

### 3.1 Sharing Between Users

- **Rider Details Shared with Driver:** When a Rider requests a trip, we share their name, pick-up/drop-off locations, rating, and profile photo with the matched Driver.
- **Driver Details Shared with Rider:** When a Driver accepts a trip, we share their name, photo, phone number (masked or via shortcode/in-app call), vehicle make/model/license plate, and real-time location with the Rider.

### 3.2 Sharing with Service Providers & Partners

We share data with third-party service providers who perform operations on our behalf, including:

- Digital payment processors and gateway providers (e.g., Telebirr, CBE Birr, Chapa).
- Cloud hosting providers (for database and application storage).
- Map and navigation services.
- SMS gateway providers for OTP verification.

### 3.3 Legal and Regulatory Disclosures

We may disclose your personal data to government officials, law enforcement agencies, or regulatory authorities (such as INSA) if we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by:

- Applicable Ethiopian laws, regulations, or licensing requirements.
- Legal processes, subpoenas, or judicial orders.
- The need to protect the rights, property, or safety of DANYABI TRADING PLC, our users, or the public.

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS & CHOICES

### 6.1 Account Modification

You can review and update your name, email, phone number, and password directly within the settings menu of the Rider or Driver Apps.

### 6.2 Location Services

You can choose whether to share your location by adjusting the privacy settings on your mobile device. However, disabling location services will prevent you from requesting rides (Riders) or receiving dispatch offers (Drivers).

### 6.3 Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → Delete Account) or at https://yaride.et/delete-account. You'll be asked to verify your phone number before the request is accepted.

Deletion cannot be started while you have a trip in progress, a positive wallet balance, or, for drivers, an outstanding balance owed to Ya Ride. Contact support to resolve these first.

Once accepted, your account enters a 30-day recovery period. You are signed out on all devices immediately and the account cannot be used during this time. You can cancel the deletion by logging in and confirming before the period ends.

After 30 days, we permanently erase the personal information we hold about you: your name, phone number, email address, profile photo, saved addresses, and any identity or vehicle documents. Where information cannot be erased without destroying another person's records — for example, messages you exchanged with a driver — we remove your contribution and retain theirs.

We retain records of trips and payments for 10 years, as required by the Commercial Code of Ethiopia and the Tax Administration Proclamation. These records are kept in a form that is not linked to your identity and cannot be used to identify you.

If your wallet has a balance when the recovery period ends, deletion is paused and we will contact you so the balance can be paid out before proceeding.

Rider and driver accounts are held separately. Deleting one does not delete the other.

This reflects your right to erasure under Article 28 of the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, subject to the exceptions in Article 28(3) for compliance with legal obligations.
